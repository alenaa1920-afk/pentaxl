1. Home

Home's job is to route people, not to say everything. Ten blocks, top to bottom.

Home / A visitor should learn what we do, see proof it is real, and find one obvious next step — inside about fifteen seconds.

A. What we need from you

Everything below is marked in the build and checked automatically before launch, so none of it can ship by accident. Nothing here blocks reviewing the site.

Open items
Ref Item On the site today What we need
A1 Company mailbox A stand-in address. The form deliberately tells people it is not delivering yet rather than dropping enquiries silently. A real mailbox on the domain. Then the form goes live in one change.
A2 Phone and WhatsApp Hidden — nothing broken, the rows simply do not appear. One number. It then shows on Contact and in the footer automatically.
A3 Tagline “Software, AI and cloud, from idea to production.” Confirm or replace. It appears in the footer, page titles and link previews.
A4 City shown publicly Country only. The city you want shown, if any.
A5 Named people “Ten engineers” and a visible note that names are being added. Two or three names, roles and links. Highest-value item on this list.
A6 Two case studies Both marked in progress and kept out of search. For each: the constraint, what was hard, two or three decisions with their trade-offs, the outcome, and what you would do differently.
A7 Service list Five disciplines. Confirm each is genuinely sold today. We delete rather than keep anything aspirational.
A8 Technology inventory A sensible default set across six clusters. Prune anything you do not actually work in — an overstated stack fails in the first technical call.
A9 Budget bands Four ranges in dollars, plus “not decided yet”. Confirm currency and bands. Wrong bands filter out good enquiries.
A10 Privacy and terms Accurate descriptions of what the site does. A legal review before launch.

B. Add-ons we can build

Deliberately not built yet, either because they need something only you can supply or because they are a separate decision.

Options
Ref Add-on What it needs from you Worth it when
B1 Client logos and quotes Written permission to publish each name, and a real attributed quote. You have two or three referenceable clients. Unverifiable logos actively cost you trust, which is why there are none today.
B2 Team page with photos Names, roles, short bios, headshots. Alongside A5 — the strongest single credibility addition.
B3 Insights or blog A commitment to publish, even quarterly. You will actually write. An abandoned blog reads worse than none.
B4 Careers page Real openings. You are hiring. It stays out of the footer until then — never a dead link.
B5 Case study screenshots and diagrams Images you are permitted to show. With A6. Shots of real software outperform any amount of copy.
B6 Booking link A calendar account. You would rather take calls than emails; sits beside the form.
B7 Second language Translated copy. You sell outside English-speaking markets. The content layer is already structured for it.
B8 Analytics A decision on privacy posture. You want traffic numbers. A cookie-free option keeps the “no cookies” promise on the privacy page intact.
